# Bench briefs (shared by all skills). Author persona: Maya Rao, head of operations at a 60-person logistics software company.

01 (failure story): Maya rolled out a new warehouse scanning app to all 12 sites at once. Two sites went down for a full day; 3,000 orders shipped late. Lesson: pilot at one site first.
02 (data insight): Analysis of 18 months of support tickets showed 41% came from the same 3 onboarding screens. Rewriting those screens cut ticket volume by a third.
03 (contrarian opinion): Most companies hold too many recurring meetings. Maya cancelled every recurring meeting for a month; only 4 of 22 came back.
04 (how-to list): How to run a vendor negotiation when you have little leverage: anchor on the vendor's renewal deadline, bundle multi-year terms, bring a credible alternative, ask for service credits not discounts.
05 (announcement): The company opened its first office in Lisbon, its first outside the US. 9 hires so far; hiring 15 more engineers and ops roles this year.
06 (personal reflection): Maya returned to work after 6 months of parental leave. What changed: she delegates more, decides faster, and stopped answering Slack after 6pm.
07 (success story): A junior analyst built a simple spreadsheet that predicted truck delays two days ahead with 80% accuracy. It now runs every morning and saved roughly $200,000 in penalties last year.
