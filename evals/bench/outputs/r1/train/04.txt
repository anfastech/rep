Most of our vendor negotiations start with the vendor holding more leverage than we do. These are the four moves I use anyway.

1. Anchor on their renewal deadline.
Their renewal date is a date they care about. Build your timeline around it and start the conversation early enough that the date does some of the work for you.

2. Bundle multi-year terms.
Offer a longer commitment as something you're giving, and ask for better terms in return for it.

3. Bring a credible alternative.
