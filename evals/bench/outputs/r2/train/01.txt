Day 1, first shift.

Tweet 5 (159 chars)

We got both sites back up.

Then I ran a post-mortem with every site lead.

The question wasn't "what broke?"

It was "why did we find out everywhere at once?"

Tweet 6 (164 chars)

The bug was small.

The blast radius was huge.

Found at 1 site, it's a bad afternoon.

Found across 12 sites, it's 3,000 late orders.

Same bug. Different rollout.

Tweet 7 (202 chars)

The lesson: pilot at 1 site first.

Not because your software is bad.

Because every warehouse is different.

Different hardware. Different Wi-Fi. Different habits.

A pilot finds what your tests can't.

Tweet 8 (146 chars)

How we roll out now:

→ 1 pilot site
→ Fix what breaks
→ 3 more sites
→ Then the rest

Slower on paper.

Much faster than a day of zero shipments.

Tweet 9 (210 chars)

TL;DR:

1. Big-bang rollouts multiply small bugs
2. Every site is its own test environment
3. Pilot at 1 site first
4. Expand in waves
5. Plan the rollback before the launch

Save this before your next rollout.
