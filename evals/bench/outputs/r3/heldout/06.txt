I've been back for a few weeks now after six months of parental leave. Some of how I work has changed, and I didn't plan any of it.

I delegate a lot more. For six months the team ran things without me, and they ran them well. Coming back and taking all of that work back would have been odd. So mostly I didn't.

I make decisions faster. I have less time, so things I used to sleep on for a few days now get decided the same afternoon. So far I haven't found a decision that was worse for it.

And I stopped answering Slack after 6pm. I expected that one to be hard. It wasn't, mostly because my evenings are spoken for now. Nothing has fallen over. The messages are still there in the morning, and by then someone has often answered them already.

I wouldn't have chosen to learn any of this by being away for six months. I'm glad I learned it anyway.

I gave each change its own short paragraph with a concrete detail rather than listing them, and I didn't turn the post into advice for other parents. Let me know if you'd like it more personal.
