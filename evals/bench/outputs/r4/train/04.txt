I negotiate vendor deals with very little leverage.

Here's the 4-step playbook I use:

Most people think small buyers can't push back.

That's wrong. You just push on different things.

1/ Anchor on their renewal deadline.

Their quarter matters to them. Use their clock, not yours.

2/ Bundle multi-year terms.

A longer deal is worth a lot to them. Make them pay for it.

3/ Bring a real alternative.

Not a bluff. A vendor you'd actually switch to.

4/ Ask for service credits, not discounts.

Credits are easier for them to give. You still win.

No big budget. No threats. Just timing and options.
