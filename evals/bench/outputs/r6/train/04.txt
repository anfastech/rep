```
You don't need much leverage to negotiate with a vendor. You need their renewal deadline.

Here's how I run a vendor negotiation when I'm the smaller side of the table:

Anchor on their renewal deadline. They need the deal closed too.

Bundle multi-year terms. Offer a longer commitment in exchange for better terms.

Bring a credible alternative. It has to be real.

Ask for service credits, not discounts.

Which of these have you tried on a vendor who wouldn't budge?
```
