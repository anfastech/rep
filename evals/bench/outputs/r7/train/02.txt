41% of our support tickets came from the same 3 onboarding screens. We'd been staffing around it for 18 months.

I went back through 18 months of support tickets. Most weren't bug reports. They were customers stuck in setup, asking what to do next.

We rewrote those three screens. Same product, new words and layout.

Support ticket volume dropped by a third.

Before you hire more support staff, read the tickets. Part of the answer may be sitting in your customer onboarding.

Save this for the next time someone asks for more support headcount.
