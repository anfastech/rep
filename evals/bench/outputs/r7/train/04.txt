Negotiating with a vendor when you have little leverage? You have four levers, and price isn't the best one.

Most vendor negotiation advice assumes you're the big customer. Usually you aren't.

1. Anchor on their contract renewal deadline. The vendor wants the renewal signed on time. Start early and let the date put pressure on them.

2. Bundle multi-year terms. A longer commitment is worth more to them than a few points of discount is to you. Trade it for what you need.

3. Bring a credible alternative. A real quote from a competitor, not a bluff. They can tell the difference.

4. Ask for service credits, not discounts. Credits tie what you pay to how well they deliver, and they're easier for a vendor to agree to.

Save this before your next renewal call.
