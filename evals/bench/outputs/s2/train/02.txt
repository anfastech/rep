41% of our support tickets came from the same 3 screens.

Not from bugs. Not from outages. From onboarding.

We pulled 18 months of support tickets and tagged every one by where the customer was when they got stuck.

I expected the load to be spread out. A bit of billing, a bit of integrations, a long tail of everything else.

It was not spread out at all.

Three onboarding screens were behind 41% of the volume. The same questions, asked again and again, by new customers who had done nothing wrong.

That changed how I saw the support queue.

We had been treating tickets as a staffing problem. More volume, more people, faster replies.

They were a product problem. The screens were asking customers to understand things we had never explained.

So we did not hire. We rewrote those three screens.

Clearer labels. Fewer choices on each step. Plain words where we had used internal terms.

Ticket volume dropped by a third.

Same product. Same customers. Same support team. Just fewer people stuck in the same place.

If your support queue keeps growing, do not start with headcount.

Start by tagging where the tickets come from. The answer is often sitting in a handful of screens you stopped looking at years ago.
