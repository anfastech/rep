It is in Lisbon.

For a 60-person company, this is a big step. Until now everyone has worked out of one country, one time zone, one set of habits.

That changes now.

We are building that on purpose, from day one, not fixing it later.

If you are in or around Lisbon and you want to work on logistics software that real warehouses and real trucks depend on every day, we would love to talk.

Engineering and operations roles are open now.

To the first 9 in Lisbon: thank you for taking the bet on us early. You are setting the tone for everyone who follows.
