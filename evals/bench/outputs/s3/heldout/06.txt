I'm back at work after six months of parental leave, and I work differently now in three ways.

I delegate more. The team ran operations for six months without me. Coming back, it made little sense to take back the decisions they had been making the whole time I was gone, so most of them stayed where they were. My job is smaller in some places, and the team's is bigger.

I decide faster. I have fewer hours at work than I used to, and I've stopped waiting for perfect information on decisions we can reverse later. Most of the calls I used to sit on for a week turned out to be the reversible kind.

I stopped answering Slack after 6pm. Evenings belong to my family now. I'd rather be fully at work during the day than half there all night, and I suspect the team prefers that too.

Before the leave, I would have called these changes risky. A head of operations who goes quiet after 6pm sounds like someone who has stopped paying attention. So far it has worked the other way. I'm paying closer attention to fewer things.

If you've come back from a long leave, what changed in how you work, and did it last?
