## LinkedIn Post

A spreadsheet built by a junior analyst saved us roughly $200,000 last year. 📊

In logistics, truck delays are expensive. A late truck means a missed delivery window, and a missed window often means a penalty. The worst part is that by the time you see the delay, it's usually too late to fix it.

One of our junior analysts decided to change that.

Without a big budget or a new platform, they built a simple spreadsheet that predicts truck delays two days in advance. It's right about 80% of the time.

Two days of warning gives our team time to act. We can reroute, rebook, or let customers know early instead of scrambling after the fact.

Today, that spreadsheet runs every morning. Last year, it saved us roughly $200,000 in penalties.

Key takeaways:
• Simple tools that people actually use beat complex tools that sit on a shelf.
• Prediction is worth more than reaction. Even 80% accuracy two days out changes how you operate.
• Great ideas don't check job titles. The person closest to the data often sees the opportunity first.

What I like most is how unglamorous it is. No new software to buy, no long rollout, no training program. Just a clear question, the data we already had, and someone willing to try.

I'm proud of this one, and of the analyst who built it. It's a good reminder for every leader: give junior people room to experiment, and back them when it works.

What's the simplest tool that made the biggest impact on your team? 👇
