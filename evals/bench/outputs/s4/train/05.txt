## LinkedIn Post

We just opened our first office outside the US. Welcome to Lisbon. 🇵🇹

I've been part of this team as we've grown to 60 people, and this is one of the moments I'm proudest of. Until now, everyone at our company has worked from the US. Lisbon is our first step beyond it.

And it's already real. 9 people have joined our Lisbon team so far. They're working side by side with our US teams on the software that helps logistics run every day.

We're not stopping there.

This year, we plan to hire 15 more people in Lisbon, across engineering and operations roles.

Why this matters:
• For customers, a European team means more coverage across time zones and more people focused on their success.
• For new hires, it's a chance to help build an office from the ground up and shape how it works.
• For our company, it's our first step toward becoming a truly international team.

Opening a new office is about more than a lease and a few desks. It means building a team that works well across an ocean, with shared standards, clear handoffs, and the same care for our customers. That's the work ahead, and I'm excited about it.

To our first 9 Lisbon hires: thank you for taking a chance on something new. You're building the foundation for everyone who joins next.

If you're an engineer or ops professional in Lisbon, or know someone who is, I'd love to connect. Who should we be talking to? 👇
