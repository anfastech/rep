# Ledger (train N=5)

| Feature | Seen | Class | Rule text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plain text, one paragraph per block, blank line between blocks | 5/5 | observed | Every paragraph is its own block, separated by one blank line |
| Paragraph size | 1 to 4 sentences, most 1 or 2, N=5 | range | 1 to 3 sentences per paragraph, 4 only inside a list item |
| Block count | 9 to 12 non-blank lines, N=5 | range | About 8 to 13 blocks |
| Length | 181 to 235 words, N=5 | range | About 160 to 260 words |
| Hook is line 1, a statement not a question | 5/5 | observed | Open with a one-line statement |
| Hook carries a concrete number or a plain admission | 4/5 number in first 2 lines; 01 admission, 04 contrarian generalization | tendency | Lead with the sharpest fact, number, or admission |
| Hook length | 11 to 21 words, N=5 | range | About 10 to 25 words |
| Two-line contrast hook (Last year / This year) | 1/5, announcement | conditional | For announcements, a before/now pair of one-line blocks may open |
| Exactly one question mark, in the closing line | 5/5 | observed | Only one question in the post; it is the close |
| Close question asks for reader's own parallel experience, often hypothetical ("If you...", "Which of your...") | 5/5 | observed | End with a specific question about the reader's own situation |
| Close question length | 12 to 19 words | range | About 10 to 22 words |
| Body arc: hook, setup (what I believed/decided), what happened with numbers, why it happened, what changed, personal cost/admission, question | 5/5 shape (04 framework variant) | observed | Follow the arc |
| Expectation vs reality beat ("I would have bet against that", "I expected...", "What I didn't expect") | 4/5 | tendency | Usually name what you expected and how it turned out |
| Honest admission/cost beat near end | 5/5 | observed | Include one line of honest cost, discomfort, or unsolved part |
| "So ..." paragraph opener for the decision | 3/5 | tendency | Usually introduce the change with "So" |
| Short standalone result line | 3/5 (02, 03, 05) | tendency | Give the key result its own short block |
| First person singular, with "we" for the team | 5/5 | observed | I for decisions, we for the company |
| Narrator is an operations leader | 5/5 | unknown (could be brief) | Persona comes from brief; sources all ops |
| Numbers as digits, specific, reused across post | 5/5 (digits 5 to 10 per post); "Two" at sentence start once, "three"/"a third" once | observed / tendency | Use digits for counts and stats; reuse the same figures |
| Contractions | 5/5 (2 to 11) | observed | Use contractions |
| Emojis | 0/5 | absent, genre often uses | Never |
| Exclamation marks | 0/5 | absent, genre often uses | Never |
| Em/en dashes | 0/5 | absent, genre often uses | Never |
| Bold, headers, quotes, parentheses, semicolons | 0/5 | absent | No markdown formatting except list markers |
| Arrow list "→ " after a colon line, for data findings | 1/5 (02) | conditional: brief is a data finding | If presenting multiple findings, intro line ending in colon then 3 arrow lines |
| Numbered list "1. " each item a paragraph: short imperative lead sentence plus 1 to 3 explanation sentences | 1/5 (04) | conditional: brief is a framework/how-to | If brief is a set of moves/steps, number them; lead line announces count ("in four moves:") |
| Framework post opens on what most people do and why it fails | 1/5 (04) | conditional | For how-to briefs, open on the common wrong move |
| Hashtags | 2/5, exactly 2, last line, after a blank line | conditional: brief supplies them (cannot verify) | Only if brief gives them; max 2, last line |
| P.S. line pointing to first comment | 1/5 (05) | conditional: brief has a link/CTA | Put links in first comment, mention via P.S. after the question |
| Links/URLs in body | 0/5 | absent | No URLs |
| Words like "lesson", "here's what I learned", "key takeaways" | 0/5 | absent (genre often uses) | Do not label the lesson; state it as a rule or decision |
| Banned words list, persona backstory, audience | not observable | unknown | |
