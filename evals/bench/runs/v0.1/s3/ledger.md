# Ledger (train N=5)

| Feature | Seen | Class | Rule text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook is its own first paragraph, 1 to 2 sentences | 5/5 | observed | Open with a standalone hook paragraph of 1 or 2 sentences |
| Hook length | 10 to 30 words, N=5 | range | About 8 to 35 words |
| Hook shape matches post type | 5/5, one per type | conditional | Mistake: what I did + its cost with a number. Data: lead with the stat. Opinion: "Unpopular opinion: " + claim. How-to: "How to X when Y:" ending in a colon. Announcement: plain "We've ..." statement |
| Body paragraphs of 1 to 4 sentences, blank line between | 5/5 | observed | Short prose paragraphs separated by one blank line; no one-sentence-per-line style |
| Total length | 181 to 251 words, N=5 | range | About 170 to 280 words |
| Non-blank lines | 6 to 9 | range | About 6 to 10 blocks |
| Last line is exactly 3 CamelCase hashtags | 5/5 | observed | End with one line of exactly 3 hashtags |
| #Operations among hashtags | 4/5 | tendency | Usually include the author's broad field tag |
| One question, the closing one, addressed to readers | 4/5; absent only in announcement (05) | conditional | Non-announcement: the only question is the closing ask about the reader's own experience |
| Announcement ends with CTA "in the comments" + thank-you line | 1/5 (05) | conditional | Announcement: CTA then a short thanks, no closing question |
| Numbered list "1." with imperative first sentence + 1 to 3 explaining sentences | 1/5 (04, how-to) | conditional | How-to only |
| "Three reasons ...:" lead-in then one paragraph per reason | 1/5 (03, opinion) | conditional | Opinion: personal test, then reasons, then "what I'd recommend instead" |
| Reframe/takeaway paragraph before the question | 5/5 | observed | Second to last body block states the lesson as a reframe (looks X, is really Y) or a myth to drop |
| Hook's key number or detail returns near the end | 4/5 (not 04) | tendency | Usually call back to the hook's number |
| Action pivot opens with "So" or "Now" | 3/5 (01, 02, 05) | tendency | Usually |
| Direct advice to reader ("If your ..., do X before Y") | 2/5 | tendency | Sometimes |
| Credibility line naming role and company size | 2/5 (04, 05) | conditional | How-to and announcement: state role/company size once |
| First person I for decisions, we/our for company | 5/5 | observed | Voice rule |
| Admits own mistake / uncertainty, hedges ("I think", "usually", "might") | 5/5 | observed | Plain, measured, no hype |
| Contractions | 1 to 7 per post, 5/5 | observed | Use natural contractions |
| Emojis | 0/5 | absent, genre often uses them | Never |
| Em/en dashes, "--" | 0/5 | absent, AI/LinkedIn text often uses them | Never |
| Exclamation marks | 0/5 | absent, genre often uses them | Never |
| Bold, headers, bullets (except how-to numbers) | 0/5 | absent | Never |
| Links in body | 0/5 | absent | Point to "the comments" instead |
| Specific numbers as digits (12, 3,000, 41%, 22) | 5/5 | observed | Use concrete figures as digits; spell out a number that starts a sentence (Nine, Three, Thirty), except a stat hook |
| Mean sentence length | 10.5 to 15.1 words | range | Short to medium sentences, none over about 32 words |
| Hyphenated compounds | 0 to 2 | tendency | Allowed |
| Banned words, persona backstory, how post type is chosen, other post types | not observable | unknown | Unknowns list |
