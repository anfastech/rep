# Ledger (train N=5: 01 failure story, 02 data finding, 03 experiment, 04 how-to list, 05 announcement)

| Feature | Seen | Class | Rule text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Package of 5 parts in fixed order: SUBJECT LINE (chosen) + Alternatives, PREVIEW TEXT, ISSUE, IMAGE SUGGESTIONS, NOTES TEASER, VOICE GUIDE USED | 5/5 | observed | Always output all parts in this order |
| Section banners `==================== NAME ====================`, two blank lines before each | 5/5 | observed | Use exact banners |
| Subject + exactly 2 numbered alternatives under "Alternatives" | 5/5 | observed | One chosen subject, two alternates with different angles |
| Subject length | 6 to 9 words, N=5 | range | About 5 to 10 words, plain, no clickbait punctuation |
| Alternatives angle: one number-led or concrete-detail line, one lesson/why/how line | 5/5 loosely | tendency | Vary the angle: number/detail vs lesson |
| Preview text | 11 to 15 words, 1 to 3 short sentences | range | About 10 to 18 words |
| H1 = chosen subject verbatim | 5/5 | observed | H1 repeats the subject line |
| Italic subtitle = preview text (01: first two sentences of it) | 5/5 | observed | Italic line under H1 restates preview text |
| Intro before [Subscribe button]: 2 to 3 paragraphs | 64 to 97 words, N=5 | range | 2 to 4 short paragraphs, about 60 to 110 words |
| Intro has one bolded key fact/claim | 5/5 | observed | Bold the key fact in the intro |
| [Subscribe button] after intro | 5/5 | observed | Fixed placeholder |
| Hook: concrete moment, number, or flat statement of the claim/news | 5/5 | observed | Open with a specific moment, number or flat claim; no warm-up |
| Hook opener varies by type: timestamped moment (01), "Last <time>" (02), "Here's an unpopular opinion:" (03), plain fact about company (04), "This one is news, so I'll say it plainly:" (05) | 1/5 each | conditional on brief type | Match opener to brief type |
| Body H2 sections | 4 to 6 body H2 + "Your turn", N=5 | range | 3 to 7 body sections |
| H2 wording: short plain sentence-case phrases, often "What ..." / "Why ..." | 5/5 | observed | Plain descriptive H2s, no clever puns |
| How-to brief: H2s numbered "1. <tactic>" plus a "Putting it together" recap list | 04 only | conditional: list/how-to brief | If brief is tactics, one numbered H2 per tactic and a recap list |
| Story arc: context, what happened, real cause/insight, what changed, result/reflection | 01,02,03 | conditional: story/experiment brief | Narrative order |
| Announcement: news stated in first line, reasons as bold lead-in paragraphs, extra CTA button `[Button: <label>]` before Share | 05 only | conditional: news/hiring brief | Add button only if brief has a link/CTA |
| Bold: one key line per section; lists use bold lead-in item titles | 6 to 10 bold spans | range/observed | Bold the one-line rule per section |
| Numbered lists for steps; bullets for examples, quotes, options | 5/5 | observed | |
| Owning a downside/mistake: "What I got wrong" / "The real mistake" | 01,03 | tendency | Name the cost or downside honestly when the brief has one |
| [Share button] then `* * *` near the end | 5/5 | observed | Fixed order |
| Takeaway line after `* * *`: "The thing to remember: ..." (4/5), "So here's the one thing to keep:" (1/5) | 5/5 one-line takeaway | observed (wording tendency 4/5) | One-sentence takeaway |
| "Try it yourself"/"Run your own version" mini section with 3-step list, after takeaway | 02,03 | conditional: replicable experiment/analysis | Add when reader can rerun it |
| `## Your turn` with a direct question + "Hit reply" (4/5; 03 "Reply with") + small promise (read every one / follow-up issue) | 5/5 | observed | |
| Forward line "If this was useful, forward it to <specific person>" | 4/5 (05 variant: forward to a fit candidate) | observed with variant | |
| Sign-off: first name alone | 5/5 | observed | |
