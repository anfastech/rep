B1 (failure): Signed a 3-year office lease two months before the team went fully remote. Could not exit; paid about $60,000 for an empty office. Takeaway: avoid long commitments while the company is still changing fast.
B2: Started doing one customer call every week personally as founder. Use it to unlock insights about the customer journey. Roadmap got much sharper and two big features were cut. Takeaway: founders should talk to customers every week.
B3 (failure): Biggest customer went quiet and stopped opening updates; founder assumed they were busy; they churned, about 30% of revenue. Takeaway: silence is a churn signal.
B4: Cut the signup form from 9 fields to 2 (email, password). Signups rose 35% in a week. Takeaway: every form field costs conversions.
