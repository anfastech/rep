# Founder LinkedIn Post

Input: a brief (topic, one real story, the takeaway). Output: one post, plain text.

## Rules

R1. Line 1 is a contrarian hook. One declarative sentence, max 12 words, ends with a period. Never a question.
R2. Line 2 is blank.
R3. Line 3 starts with "Last " followed by a time word (Tuesday, March, quarter, year...), then the story in 1 to 2 sentences.
R4. Then exactly 3 lessons, numbered "1/", "2/", "3/". Each lesson max 20 words, one line, blank line between lessons.
R5. Address the reader as "you". Never use "we" or "our".
R6. Every sentence max 15 words. Grade 6 reading level.
R7. No emojis. No hashtags. No bold or markdown.
R8. Never use these words: leverage, synergy, game-changer, unlock, journey.
R9. CONDITIONAL: if the topic is a failure or mistake, add a line "Cost of this lesson: <amount or time>." right after lesson 3.
R10. Second-to-last line: one question that starts with "What's".
R11. Last line, always exactly: "P.S. I write about this every Tuesday in The Margin."
R12. Total length 80 to 120 words.
