Your biggest customer is not your safest customer.

Last winter, my largest account stopped opening product updates. I told myself they were just busy.

1/ Track silence as a warning sign. Quiet accounts leave without a complaint.

2/ Call your top five customers every month. Do not wait for renewal.

3/ Ask what they would miss if you vanished tomorrow. Listen for long pauses.

Cost of this lesson: 30 percent of revenue in one email.

What's the quiet signal you are ignoring right now?
