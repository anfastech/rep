Every field in your signup form costs you customers.

Last Friday, I cut my signup form from nine fields to two. Signups rose by 35 percent in a week.

1/ Ask only for an email and a password. Collect the rest later.

2/ Every extra question is a reason to close the tab.

3/ Move company size and role into onboarding. People answer once they see value.

What's the one field you could delete today?
