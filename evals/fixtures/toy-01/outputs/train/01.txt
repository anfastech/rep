Hiring senior sales early will slow your startup down.

Last March, I hired a VP of Sales at 400k ARR. He left after five months with zero new playbooks.

1/ You need to close the first 50 deals yourself. That is where the playbook comes from.

2/ A senior seller needs a machine to run. Do not hire one to build it.

3/ Hire a scrappy first rep who likes chaos. Promote later.

Cost of this lesson: $180,000 and half a year.

What's the role you hired too early?
