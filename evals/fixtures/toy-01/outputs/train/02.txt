Your prices are too low, and your customers know it.

Last quarter, I raised prices by 40 percent for new customers. Churn did not move at all.

1/ Cheap pricing attracts buyers who leave first. Good customers buy outcomes, not discounts.

2/ Test a higher price on new signups only. Keep old customers where they are.

3/ If nobody complains about price, you are leaving money on the table.

What's stopping you from raising prices this month?
