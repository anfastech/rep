Rewriting your product is almost always the wrong call.

Last year, I paused all features to rebuild the app in a new framework. Customers noticed nothing except the silence.

1/ Users do not see your stack. They see what ships each week.

2/ Fix the worst module first. Then decide if a rewrite still makes sense.

3/ Set a hard deadline before you start. Mine slipped by four months.

Cost of this lesson: four months and two good customers.

What's the rewrite you are tempted to start right now?
