Cold email templates are killing your reply rate.

Last Tuesday, I sent 30 short personal videos instead of a template. Eleven people replied in two days.

1/ Say the prospect's name and one real detail in the first five seconds.

2/ Keep each video under 60 seconds. Busy people still finish short clips.

3/ Ask for one small thing, like a 10 minute call. Not a demo.

What's the one detail you could mention in your next outreach?
