Interviews are a weak way to judge a remote hire.

Last month, I swapped the second interview for a paid writing task. The quality of hires jumped right away.

1/ Remote work runs on writing. Test the skill you will rely on every day.

2/ Pay for the task. Good people value their time, and so should you.

3/ Read the work before the resume. Bias drops when names come later.

What's the one skill your hiring process never tests?
